### 7. Data Security and Retention
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We also retain your data only for as long as necessary—no longer.
#### 7.1 Data Security Measures
We use a combination of technical, organizational, and physical safeguards:
- Encryption: All data in transit (e.g., Input you upload) is encrypted via TLS 1.3; all data at rest (e.g., stored on servers) is encrypted via AES-256.
- Access Controls: Only authorized employees (e.g., customer support, engineers) can access your data, and they must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) and role-based permissions (e.g., support staff cannot access your raw voice recordings).
- Security Audits: We conduct annual third-party security audits (e.g., penetration testing, SOC 2 compliance) to identify and fix vulnerabilities.
- Breach Response: If a data breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights, we will: (1) notify you via your registered email/phone within 72 hours of discovering the breach; (2) provide details about the breach (e.g., what data was exposed, steps to protect yourself); (3) report the breach to relevant authorities (e.g., GDPR supervisory bodies, Chinese Cyberspace Administration) as required by law.
#### 7.2 Data Retention Periods
We retain your data only for the time needed to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, plus any legal retention requirements. Specific periods include:
1. Account Information:
- Retention Period: While your account is active + 30 days post-deletion (to complete final processes like refund requests).
2. User Input:
- Retention Period: For active accounts: 1 year after last use (e.g., if you don’t use a translated document for 1 year, we delete it);For deleted accounts: 30 days post-deletion (full permanent erase).
3. Automated Usage Data:
- Retention Period: 6 months (then anonymized—we delete identifiers like your device ID, so the data can no longer be linked to you).
4. Payment Records:
- Retention Period: 7 years (to comply with tax and financial regulations).
5. Guardian Consent Data:
- Retention Period: 6 months after the minor’s account is deleted (to prove compliance with child privacy laws).

After the retention period ends, we either: (1) permanently delete the data (using industry-standard methods like overwriting); or (2) anonymize the data so it can no longer identify you (e.g., removing names from text Input) and use it for statistical purposes (e.g., analyzing translation accuracy across languages).
